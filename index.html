About

I'm Afraim Murshed, a Computer Science & Engineering student and technology-focused professional interested in how software is built, secured, and verified to actually do what it promises.

My path into tech started on the ground floor — working as a Fulfillment Associate at Amazon, where I saw first-hand how large-scale, technology-driven systems keep a business running. That experience sharpened my interest in the layer underneath the process: the software, the infrastructure, and the checks that keep it reliable.

Through QA and software testing training with TripleTen, I've been building a rigorous, detail-oriented approach to verifying that systems behave the way they're meant to. I'm continuing my technical foundation through Computer Science & Engineering coursework, with a growing focus on cybersecurity and web development.

I'm drawn to the intersection of technology and reliability: building things that work, and then proving that they do.
